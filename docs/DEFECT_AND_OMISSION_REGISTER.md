# Defect & Omission Register — Blank-Slate Audit & Remediation

This register documents the concrete defects identified during the APK & asset bundle audit (Section 2.2 of the specification) and the exact engineering fixes verified by unit and Robolectric tests.

| ID | Subsystem | Audit Finding / Root Cause | Remediation Implemented | Verification Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DEF-01** | `assets/forecast_bundle/manifest.json` | Previous manifest declared `model_tflite_sha256` pointing to the exact SHA-256 of `weights_compact.json`, while no `.tflite` file existed in the APK and actual execution ran `CompactKotlinForecastRuntime`. | Removed misleading `model_tflite_sha256`; added `weights_compact_sha256` and explicit `"artifact_format": "WEIGHTS_COMPACT_JSON_INT8"` across Python exporter (`export_litert.py`), `ForecastModelBundle`, and `ForecastModelLoader`. | `test_09_bundle_integrity_and_fixture_tags` (Python) & `forecastModelLoader_verifiesBundleChecksumsGoldenVectorsAnd4hHeadConsistency` (Robolectric) |
| **DEF-02** | `ml/forecasting/src/calibrate.py` & `calibration.json` | Unregularized temperature scaling on a 23-sample validation split (`sample_size = 23` where all 23 4h labels were `DOWN`) fit `temperature = 0.25` and `bias_shift = [1.30578, -2.51091, -2.57841]`, amplifying the prior shift 4x and collapsing 4h calibrated probabilities to `[1.0, 0.0, 0.0]`. | Bounded temperature in `[0.75, 2.50]`, shrank prior shifts by `min(1.0, n / 200.0)` with hard clip `[-0.22, +0.22]`, and tagged `qualification_status = "INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE_SHADOW_ONLY"` whenever `sample_size < 200`. | `test_05_regularized_calibration_and_sample_qualification` (Python) & `calibrationEngine_preventsDegenerateTemperatureCollapseAndDetectsHeadInconsistency` (JUnit) |
| **DEF-03** | `train_gru.py`, `train_tcn.py`, `ForecastCalibrationEngine.kt`, `ForecastQualityGate.kt` | Classification head (`pDown`, `pFlat`, `pUp`) and regression/quantile head (`expectedReturnPct`, `q50Pct`) were fitted independently without cross-head coupling, allowing 4h `pDown = 1.0` while `expectedReturnPct = +0.1092%`. | Coupled `w_ret` with `W_cls[UP] - W_cls[DOWN]` during training/export and added `checkHeadConsistency` in both Python and Kotlin. Any contradiction sets `ForecastStatus.HEAD_INCONSISTENT` (`headConsistent = false`) and blocks execution. | `test_06_head_consistency_detector` (Python) & `qualityGate_blocksExecutionOnHeadInconsistencyOrInsufficientSample` (JUnit) |
| **DEF-04** | `export_litert.py` & `ForecastModelRegistry.kt` | Manifest claimed support for `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`, `SOLUSDT`, and `DOGEUSDT`, while `golden_feature_vectors.json` only contained `BTCUSDT` samples and no per-symbol eligibility distinction existed. | Exported golden vectors and parity-verified golden outputs across all 4 symbols (`BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`, `SOLUSDT`, `DOGEUSDT`) and added `SymbolModelEligibility` (`FULL_SUPPORTED`, `POOLED_MODEL_ONLY`, `BASELINE_ONLY`, `UNSUPPORTED`). | `test_09_bundle_integrity_and_fixture_tags` (Python) & `forecastModelLoader_verifiesBundleChecksumsGoldenVectorsAnd4hHeadConsistency` (Robolectric) |
| **DEF-05** | `ml/forecasting/src/evaluate.py` | Backtest loop multiplied equity on bars even when no trade was opened or needed clear separation between single-trade event simulation and non-traded bars. | Refactored `evaluate.py` with `simulate_single_trade_event` (conservative STOP_LOSS priority when both SL and TP are touched in the same bar) and `evaluate_predictions` that strictly leaves equity unchanged on non-traded bars (`trade_Action == "HOLD"`). | `test_07_same_candle_sl_tp_conservative_resolution` & `test_08_non_traded_bars_never_compound_equity` (Python) |
| **DEF-06** | `QuantMathEngine.kt` | Small-capital accounts ($50–$150) risking 0.50% ($0.25–$0.75) on high-minNotional contracts (`BTCUSDT` `minNotional = $100`) risked silent upward rounding to `minNotional`, multiplying actual loss risk 3x–10x above `R`. | `QuantMathEngine.calculatePositionSizing` rounds quantity strictly `DOWN` to `stepSize` and explicitly **rejects** any trade where `finalNotional < max(minNotional, 5.0) * 1.05`. | `quantMathEngine_rejectsTradeWhenMinNotionalExceedsLossBudget` (JUnit) |
| **DEF-07** | `AstraViewModel.kt` & `AstraTradingCoordinator.kt` | Bundled fixture data or offline fallback bars could be confused with verified production history. | Implemented `DataTrustState` state machine, tagged all fixtures with `isTestFixture = true` / `TEST_FIXTURE`, and enforced Blank-Slate startup with zero fake trades or positions. | `mainActivity_rendersBlankSlateSafetyLockAndForecastCardOnS7EdgeViewport` (Robolectric) |
